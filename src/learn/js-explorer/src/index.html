An interactive field guide to the JavaScript language and the browser's major Web APIs. Hover any row for the mechanism on the right — click a card to open its live demo in the middle.
Tip: a few of these (IndexedDB, Clipboard, Geolocation, Notifications) need a "secure context" — they work best served over http(s) rather than opened directly as a file:// path. From this folder: python3 -m http.server or npx serve, then open the printed localhost URL. Everything else works fine opened directly.
